01 Payment (50% upfront)

02 Refunds

Refunds are not available once I have started working on your order. Significant time and effort go into each order, and last-minute cancellations are unfair to me as a designer.

03 Age requirement

  • You must be 12 years or older to place an order.

  • Discord's Terms of Service also apply when communicating with me on Discord.

04 Respect & communication
