Privacy Policy

The short version: this is a simple portfolio site with no accounts, no contact form and no analytics. Here is what still happens with your data.

01 Who I am

This website belongs to Bcraft Designs, a Roblox graphics (GFX) commission service. Bcraft Designs is responsible for the personal data described on this page.

Contact: bcraftforbusiness@gmail.com

02 Visiting this website

This is a simple static website. There are no accounts, no contact form, no ads and no analytics, and the site itself doesn't set cookies.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so when you open a page, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser type. You can read how GitHub handles that in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Some things on the pages are loaded straight from other companies' servers, which means they also see your IP address when the page loads:

  • Google Fonts for the site's fonts.
  • Images from other sites: client avatars next to reviews (onecdn.io) and the payment-method logos on the pricing page (Roblox, Amazon Web Services, PayPal and PCWorld servers).
  • A snow effect from app.embed.im, only while the Christmas theme is switched on.

X posts. The homepage and the News page can show posts from X. If a post is shown as an X embed, nothing is loaded from X until you click “Load post” (or “Load all posts”). Only then does X's script (platform.twitter.com) run. X then receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies, as described in X's Privacy Policy. The embed asks X not to track you (“do not track”). If you never click, nothing is sent to X.

I only use this to show you the website. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

03 Stored in your browser

When a sale popup appears and you close it, your choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so it doesn't keep popping up. If you close the holiday countdown bar at the top of the page, that choice is kept in session storage and is cleared when you close the tab.

Both stay on your device, aren't sent to me, and you can delete them in your browser settings.

04 Links to other platforms

The site links to Roblox, YouTube, X, GitHub and Discord. Nothing is sent to them just because you view a page (the X posts described above only load after you click). Once you click a link, you're on their site and their privacy policy applies. The Discord link goes through discordid.netlify.app, which forwards you to Discord.

05 When you message me

Commissions happen through external platforms (for example Discord or X), not through this website. When you contact me, I receive what you send: your username, your messages, reference images or files, and details about your order, including which payment method you plan to use.

I use this to reply, quote, make and deliver your commission, and keep a record of the order. The legal basis is steps before and performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or my legitimate interest in handling enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)). The platform you message me on also handles your messages under its own privacy policy.

Orders are also covered by the Terms of Service, including the age requirement.

06 Client reviews

Reviews on this site show the reviewer's name or username, avatar and review text. If you're one of them and want yours changed or removed, message me.

07 Who gets your data

I don't sell your data. It goes only to the providers named above (GitHub, Google and the other image and font hosts, X if you load a post, and the platform you message me on), and to authorities if the law requires it. Some of these companies are based in the United States. How they handle data transfers is covered by their own privacy policies.

08 Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how I use it, or to hand it to you in a portable format. You can also object to anything I do based on legitimate interest. To do any of this, contact me at bcraftforbusiness@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

09 Changes
