Terms of Service

Before we chat

Every commission follows these terms. Clear expectations, better commissions.

  1. Payment

    50% upfront before I start. It protects against scams and values my time.

  2. Refunds

    No refunds once work has started or is halfway done.

  3. Age

    You must be 12 or older. Transfers (PayPal, bank) need a parent or guardian's permission. Discord's own Terms also apply there.

  4. Respect & communication

    Be respectful, rude behavior isn't tolerated. Follow the NoHello method: get to the point instead of just saying "Hi."

  5. Delays

    They can happen. I'll tell you, and may offer a discount depending on how long.

  6. Cancellation

    I can cancel an order if the work looks like it will be used inappropriately or I'm uncomfortable with the request. If I cancel after payment, you get a refund.

  7. Character limits

    Maximum characters per order:

    • Profile pictures 1
    • Thumbnails 3
    • Commission sheets 1
    • Ads 2
    • Game icons 2
    • Renders 1
    • YouTube thumbnails 2
    • YouTube banners 2

    Extra characters: 789 Robux or $3 each.
